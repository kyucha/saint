    • memories don't last forever

    Shifting reminisce is a constant theme for Saint Miyale over the course of his six track EP - Memories don’t last forever. Saint layers a jaded tone over icy samples and hip hop production to bind a fading past with realities of the present. Standout track Origami feels like moment you open your eyes after a deep sleep, assessing that you’re still here and so is the world. This entirely self produced EP flashes between appreciation for cultural moments that shaped him as well as acceptance for some things that can never change.
