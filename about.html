    • Santino “Saint” Miyale is a Brooklyn based composer, producer, and vocalist. He is inspired by his working class rust-belt roots, and the cold and desolate spaces he grew up existing in.

    Saint has worked on various commercial scores in addition to his solo music projects. His work often fuses elements from hip hop and ambient music.
